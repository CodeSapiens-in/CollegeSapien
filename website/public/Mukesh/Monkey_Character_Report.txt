1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The "Word-Stealing Monkey" is an interactive, gamified visual feature designed 
for the CollegeSapien homepage. The core concept involves dynamic animated 
monkeys spawning across various sections, heights, and sizes on the page, 
playfully "stealing" letters/words from headlines or body text, performing fun 
acrobatic animations with the stolen letters, responding to single and double-tap 
user interactions, displaying angry emotion animations or Tamil dialogue bubbles 
("Valikudhu bro"), and safely returning the text back to its original DOM position.

This feature adds personality, delightful micro-interactions, high user 
engagement, and a memorable Neo-brutalist aesthetic aligned with CollegeSapien's brand.


2. CHARACTER STRUCTURE (ANATOMY & ARTWORK HIERARCHY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Visual Anatomy Breakdown:
  - Head & Face:
    * Expressive eyes (Normal blinking, cheeky wink, dizzy x_x state, angry >_< slanted, sad ouch eyes).
    * Dynamic mouth (Grating smile, tongue sticking out, shock gasp, angry frown/teeth grit, ouch mouth).
    * Ears (Twitch animation during idle, state changes, or angry steam venting).
  - Body & Limbs:
    * Torso (Scalable vector/sprite body with optional CollegeSapien badge/vest).
    * Flexible Arms & Hands (Gripping mechanism capable of holding stolen letters).
    * Agile Legs & Feet (Supports walking, scampering, somersaults, landing, angry stomping).
    * Prehensile Tail (Used for hanging from section headers, balancing, or hooking letters).

2.2 Size & Height Variations (Multi-Monkey Hierarchy):
  - Micro Monkeys (Small - Scale 0.5x):
    * Spawns on high elements (top banners, floating badges, navigation bars).
    * Fast, agile, steals individual characters or punctuation.
  - Standard Monkeys (Medium - Scale 1.0x):
    * Spawns across feature section headers and card titles.
    * Steals short words (3-6 characters), carries them over shoulders.
  - Chonky / Giant Monkeys (Large - Scale 1.5x):
    * Spawns near major hero sections or footer areas.
    * Steals entire key phrases, walks slowly with exaggerated funny heavy-load animations.

2.3 Personality Variants (Randomized per Spawn):
  - Variant A [Standard Bonk Monkey]: Responds to hits with classic dizzy stars and scared flee.
  - Variant B [Angry Emotion Monkey]: On double-tap, turns red with angry eyebrows (>\ /<), emits anger sparks (💢), stomps for 1.8s, turns normal, and runs away fast.
  - Variant C [Dialogue "Valikudhu bro" Monkey]: On double-tap, displays a Neo-brutalist speech bubble saying "Valikudhu bro" with sad ouch eyes for 1.8s, then scurries away.

2.4 Layering & Z-Index Management:
  - Background Layer (z-index: 10): Behind section cards, peeking from borders.
  - Content Interaction Layer (z-index: 100): Overlaps text lines during theft.
  - Floating Overlay Layer (z-index: 9999): Active stolen letters & speech bubbles.


3. ANIMATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Cute Walk & Scamper Cycle:
  - Smooth 2D walk cycle across DOM boundaries (top of text, section borders).
  - Paws tip-toeing effect with subtle bobbing motion.
  - Tail swaying in sync with gait momentum.

3.2 Word / Letter Theft Action:
  - "Grab & Pluck": Monkey reaches towards target text node, plucks a letter, causing a visual "pop" / scale-bounce on the letter sprite.
  - "Snatch & Lift": Quick two-handed lift movement, severing the letter from the text flow.

3.3 Carrying Animations:
  - Overhead Carry: Holds letters high above head while walking.
  - Drag & Pull: Drags heavier/larger words behind it.
  - Tail Hook Carry: Wraps tail around letter while doing a handstand.

3.4 Somersault ("Summer sault") Flip:
  - 360-degree mid-air rotation while holding stolen letters.
  - Triggered during transitions between text lines or after successfully snatching a word.
  - Includes motion blur / trailing particle effect.

3.5 Making Funny Faces (Cheeky Expressions):
  - Tongue-Out Taunt: Stops mid-path, faces viewer, sticks out tongue while wiggling ears.
  - Silly Eye-Roll & Wink: Looks at user cursor, winks playfully before scurrying away.
  - Giggle Animation: Body shakes slightly with laughing mouth frame.

3.6 "Get Beaten" Single-Tap Animation:
  - Trigger: Direct single click/tap on an active Monkey.
  - "Bonk / Hit" Effect: Visual pop star (*BANG*, *BONK*, dizzy stars orbiting head).
  - Shock / Ouch Face: Eyes change to x_x, mouth opens in comical shock.
  - Drop & Return: Monkey immediately lets go of stolen letters, falling back to their original slots.
  - Retreat Scamper: Monkey tumbles, runs off-screen at double speed, and despawns.

3.7 Double-Tap Special Emotion & Dialogue Animations:
  - Trigger: Double click/tap within 380ms on an active Monkey.
  - Angry Emotion State (Selective Monkeys):
    * Body turns fiery red with rapid shaking.
    * Face changes to fierce angry eyebrows (>\_/<) and clenched teeth mouth.
    * Anger particle symbols (💢) & red sparks burst above head.
    * Lasts 1.8 seconds in place -> returns to normal state -> runs away super fast off-screen.
  - "Valikudhu bro" Dialogue Speech Bubble (Selective Monkeys):
    * Displays a Neo-brutalist white & yellow speech bubble popup above head.
    * Text reads: "Valikudhu bro" (Tamil: "It hurts bro!").
    * Eyes change to teardrop / ouch face (> <).
    * Lasts 1.8 seconds -> drops letter -> turns normal and runs away from the place.


4. ACTIONS & WORKFLOW PIPELINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Target Identification & Text Wrapping:
  - Engine scans eligible visible headings (e.g., <h1>, <h2>, .feat-t).
  - Target words are dynamically wrapped into <span class="ch"> elements without altering page layout.

4.2 Theft Sequence (DOM -> Floating Element):
  Step 1: Monkey approaches target word coordinates.
  Step 2: Reaches out -> target span marked stolen -> letter rendered inside Monkey's active hand container.
  Step 3: Text placeholder retains original bounding box dimensions.

4.3 Holding & Roaming Phase:
  - Monkey roams along pre-computed path for 4-8 seconds.
  - Performs random sub-animations (Somersault, Funny Face, Cute Walk).

4.4 Return & Re-insertion Protocol:
  Step 1: Timer expires or monkey completes route.
  Step 2: Monkey navigates back to target text slot (or throws word back like a boomerang).
  Step 3: Letter snaps back into DOM span with bounce particle effect -> span unhidden -> monkey waves goodbye and despawns.

4.5 Single-Tap vs Double-Tap Pipeline:
  - Single Tap: Increments bonk score -> Triggers Dizzy state -> Drops stolen letter -> Runs off-screen.
  - Double Tap (<380ms interval):
    * Evaluates assigned personality type (Variant A, B, or C).
    * If Variant B: Triggers ANGRY emotion state (1.8s) -> Turns normal -> Fast escape.
    * If Variant C: Triggers DIALOGUE state ("Valikudhu bro") -> (1.8s) -> Fast escape.
    * Drops any held letter immediately back to board via physics flight trajectory.


5. BEHAVIOURS & SYSTEM RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Spawning Rules & Cooldowns:
  - Maximum concurrent monkeys on screen: 3 (1 Large, 1 Medium, 1 Small).
  - Spawn Interval: Random timer between 15s - 45s.
  - Scroll Awareness: Monkeys only spawn on sections currently inside the user's viewport.
  - Personality Assignment: Random 3-way distribution (33% Standard, 33% Angry, 33% Dialogue).

5.2 Accessibility & Safety Rules (Crucial):
  - Text Accessibility (a11y): Original text remains intact for Screen Readers.
  - Layout Preservation: Space preservation prevents layout shifting.
  - Emergency Reset: If tab loses focus or user scrolls rapidly, all stolen words immediately revert to DOM.

5.3 Interactive Dynamics:
  - Cursor Proximity Reaction: If cursor gets within 50px, monkey moves faster / looks nervous.
  - Multi-Tap Combo: Double tapping unlocks unique expressions and dialog bubbles per monkey personality.


6. CHARACTER STRUCTURE SPECIFICATION (TECHNICAL SUMMARY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
State Machine Enum:
  - SEEK             : Moving towards target text element or return slot.
  - FLEE             : Moving towards exit after stealing/returning letter.
  - DIZZY            : Single-tap hit reaction with spinning stars.
  - SCARED           : Fleeing off-screen post-hit/angry/dialogue state.
  - ANGRY            : Double-tap reaction (red glow, 💢 anger symbols, angry face, 1.8s pause before escape).
  - DIALOGUE         : Double-tap reaction (Speech bubble popup "Valikudhu bro", ouch eyes, 1.8s pause before escape).

Performance Specifications:
  - Render Technique: HTML5 Canvas overlay or SVG Lightweight Animations.
  - FPS Target: 60 FPS via RequestAnimationFrame.
  - Memory Management: Garbage collection of despawned monkey instances and event listeners.


7. CONCLUSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This updated specification incorporates the requested double-tap interactions, 
angry emotion animations, and the "Valikudhu bro" dialogue bubble feature while 
preserving non-uniform monkey personality traits across the CollegeSapien homepage.
