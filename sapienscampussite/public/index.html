How this works & your privacy +

Original prompts, inspired by RIASEC. The six interest areas are Realistic, Investigative, Artistic, Social, Enterprising and Conventional. These 30 activity prompts and the persona suggestions were created for SapiensCampus. This is not the O*NET Interest Profiler and it has not been validated as an assessment.

Simple, visible scoring. Five prompts per area. “Not for me” scores 0, “Maybe” scores 1, and “I’d love it” scores 2. Each area has a score from 0 to 10. Equal scores remain tied; letters inside brackets are equal, not ranked. Low or broad patterns do not get a forced, specific persona. Suggestions are editorial starting points, not calculated occupation matches.

Your answers stay here. Quiz choices are stored in this browser tab to help you resume after a refresh. Nothing is uploaded until you join the waitlist; then your email, signup consent and (if you finished the quiz) your 30 answers and area scores are saved. Use “Start again” to clear your answers.
